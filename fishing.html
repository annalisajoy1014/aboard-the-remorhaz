The Deep Watch

You find Haldor at the starboard rail in the grey pre-dawn, watching the black water with the patience of a man who has done this a thousand times. He hands you a rope without looking at you.

"Watch the water," he says quietly. "Not the net, not yer hands — the water. It'll tell ye when."
